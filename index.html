Research insight

Recent experimental work (Damak & Varanasi, Sci. Adv. 2018) demonstrates that active electrostatic fog harvesting can overcome the primary aerodynamic limits of traditional mesh collectors. By injecting a space charge into fog droplets via an ion emitter and imposing an electric field, droplets acquire a net charge and are directed along field lines toward grounded collectors.

This mechanism addresses two key limitations of mesh collectors: low field efficiencies observed in the field (often ~1–2%) and the aerodynamic deviation of droplets around wires. Laboratory setups have shown mesh improvements up to ~10%, but electrostatic control enables substantially higher deposition by forcing droplets to follow electric field lines rather than airflow streamlines.

Source: Maher Damak and Kripa K. Varanasi, "Electrostatically driven fog collection using space charge injection," Sci. Adv. 2018.
